Add Content

This page writes straight into the site's database — nothing you add here needs Google Drive or any extra step. Fill a form, submit, and it appears live on the site (a refresh on the student side will show it).

Your account isn't an admin, so you can't add content. Add your email to ADMIN_EMAILS in the backend's .env and sign up again (or ask whoever set that up).

1. Add a subject

A subject is what shows up as a card on the student dashboard, e.g. "Engineering Mechanics".

2. Add a unit to a subject

A unit is a chapter inside a subject, e.g. "Unit 1: Force Systems". Every note/video/PYQ you upload belongs to a unit.

3. Add a resource (note / video / PYQ / practice question / book / important topic)

This is the actual material. Paste a link to the material: a Google Drive share link (Anyone with the link → Viewer) or a YouTube URL.

1. Add a lab

A lab shows up as a card on the student Lab Companion page, e.g. "EVS lab".

2. Add a file to a lab

Same idea as Resource Hub: paste a Google Drive share link or a YouTube URL. Just one field per file — no objective/procedure/viva sections.

This shows everything across every semester, not just the one selected above — if something you uploaded is "missing" from the student site, check here first: it's almost always sitting under a different semester than your own account.
